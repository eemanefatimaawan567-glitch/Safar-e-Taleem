Tool 1: Safar-e-Taleem (Safe-Walking Groups)How it works: 
Instead of paying for a van, parents open the app and type their neighborhood (e.g., "G-11, Islamabad").The AI Part: 
The AI instantly groups (clusters) 4 or 5 families who live within 1 kilometer of each other and go to the same school.The Result: 
The app creates a "Walking School Bus" where parents take turns walking the group of children safely to school together. Fuel cost = 0 PKR.
Our AI is smart. If the school is close, it builds a walking group.
 If the school is far, it instantly switches to motorcycle or car-pool sharing to make sure fuel costs are cut regardless of distance.
