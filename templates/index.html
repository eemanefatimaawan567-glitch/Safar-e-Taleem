AI Walking Groups

DBSCAN clustering automatically groups families living close to each other and attending the same school, helping parents coordinate walking groups and carpools.

Live Fuel Monitoring

Live petrol-price monitoring helps schools understand rising commute pressure and test hybrid scheduling responses.

Roman-Urdu Voice AI

Parents can ask practical transport and education questions using the Ask Ammi/Abba assistant powered by Alibaba Cloud Qwen.

Live Safety Tracking

Temporary commute-location sharing and SOS alerts help parents and school principals monitor active school journeys.

Mohallah Study Pods

Nearby families with a suitable device can support children from households without one during hybrid learning days.

WhatsApp / SMS / IVR

Low-data learning delivery provides alternative channels for families who cannot reliably join high-bandwidth online classes.

Hackathon Demo Guide — Click to expand the Golden Path walkthrough

Follow these steps in order for the best demo experience:

1
Principal Dashboard — Click "Principal Demo" above. Explore transport analytics, fuel monitoring, Study Pods and the hybrid-shift predictor.
2
Simulate a Petrol Spike — Open the Hackathon Interactive Panel and drag the petrol-price slider above the configured threshold. The dashboard shows the recommended hybrid response.
3
Generate Offline Packets — Explore Primary, Middle and Secondary curriculum packets for low-connectivity households.
4
WhatsApp / SMS Delivery — Select a learning level and explore the simulated low-data delivery workflow.
5
Parent Dashboard + Voice AI — Return to the homepage and open "Parent Demo" . Ask Ammi/Abba a transport question in Roman Urdu.
6
Live Safety Tracking — Click "Start Sharing" on the Parent Dashboard. Explore the commute map and SOS workflow.
7
Try Real Registration — Return to the homepage and click "Register Family" to see the real Safar-e-Taleem registration and address-entry journey.
