AI Walking Groups

DBSCAN clustering automatically groups families within 1 km. Parents take turns walking kids to school — zero fuel cost, 100% safe.

Live Fuel Monitoring

Real-time PSO petrol price tracking with automatic alerts. When prices spike, principals can switch to hybrid schedules in one click.

Roman-Urdu Voice AI

Parents tap a mic button and speak in Urdu. Alibaba Qwen AI processes their voice and speaks back practical, localized solutions.

Live Safety Tracking

Real-time GPS tracking with SOS alerts. Pod-mates and coordinators see live locations during the school commute.

Mohallah Study Pods

Matches device-owning families with 3 nearby non-owners. Kids share a single screen for hybrid/online learning days.

WhatsApp / SMS / IVR

Curriculum content pushed to families without devices via low-data channels — so no child misses out on online learning days.

Hackathon Demo Guide — Click to expand the Golden Path walkthrough

Follow these steps in order for the best demo experience:

1
Principal Dashboard (normal state) — Click "Demo: Principal" above. Dashboard shows stable petrol prices (Rs 343/L), transport groups, study pods, and analytics.
2
Trigger Petrol Spike — Click "Trigger Spike (Rs 380/L)" in the yellow Demo Mode panel. Price jumps, alert card turns red, hybrid schedule auto-activates.
3
Generate Offline Packets — Scroll to Offline Learning Packets. Download Primary/Middle/Secondary curriculum PDFs for families without devices.
4
WhatsApp/SMS Broadcast — Select a level, click "Send via WhatsApp". Check the Delivery Log to see simulated messages sent to unmatched families.
5
Parent Dashboard + Voice AI — Go back and click "Demo: Parent". Open the Ask Ammi/Abba assistant. Tap the mic and speak: "Petrol mehnga ho gaya, school kaise jayein?" — AI responds in Roman-Urdu.
6
Live Safety Tracking — On parent dashboard, click "Start Sharing". Watch the GPS marker walk along a realistic route toward school. Click SOS to trigger an emergency alert.
7
Reset & Repeat — Back on principal, click "Reset to Normal" to restore prices and deactivate hybrid for a clean restart.
